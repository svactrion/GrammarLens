# 1.5.0 store texts — proposal for the owner

Written 2026-11-21 in the owner's planning chat. **A proposal: not reviewed by the owner, not uploaded.** The machine-readable copy for an upload is `docs/1.5.0-store-metadata.json` (same shape as `screenshots/1.4.0/asc/metadata.json`); the Notes for Review are `docs/1.5.0-review-notes.txt` (3,977 characters, the limit is 4,000).

## What changed against 1.4.0, in every language

- What's New: new text.
- Description: the "now in your language" heading loses "now"; Topic Practice lists ten topics; two new sections after the mountain (Home Screen widgets, daily reminders); sixteen study buddies → nine; Premium unlocks all ten topics (was five areas). Everything else is 1.4.0's text, unchanged.
- Unchanged: subtitle, keywords (none of the new topics added; the owner's call), promotional text (blank), Support and Privacy Policy URLs.
- Topic names stay in English in every language, as in 1.4.0; the widget names "Climb" and "Tip" are English in the widget gallery in every language, so the texts quote them in English.
- Settings paths use the app's own labels (Profile → Notifications in each language's `notifTitle`).
- The reminder claim is only what is sure from the code: at most one a day, at a time the user picks. Not claimed: "only if you haven't practised yet".

## Description, English (full)

```text
GrammarLens turns your English mistakes into practice.

Every day you get a short grammar test. When you finish, you don't just see a score — you see why each answer was right or wrong, written in plain language.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The app's menus and buttons are available in English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Arabic and German. The questions and the feedback stay in English, so you practice the language you are learning.

WHAT YOU GET

Daily Test
A fresh set of questions every day. Fill in the blanks, correct the errors, and get an explanation for every answer.

Real explanations, not just checkmarks
Instead of "wrong", you get the reasoning: which rule applies, why your answer doesn't fit, and what to remember next time. The words that changed between your answer and the correction are highlighted.

Topic hints
Stuck on a question? Slide to see the grammar pattern for its topic — a reminder of the rule, never the answer.

Your weak spots, tracked
The app remembers which grammar points you keep missing and collects them in Review, so you always know what to work on.

Topic Practice
Choose from ten topics — Articles, Tense Selection, Modal Verbs, Modal Past Forms, Gerund vs. Infinitive, Conditionals, Passive Voice, Prepositions, Relative Clauses, and Comparatives & Superlatives — with sessions of 3, 5 or 10 questions.

Mountain of Learning
Every Daily Test takes your character one step up this month's mountain. Earn medals for the month and build a habit that sticks.

Home Screen widgets
"Climb" shows today's Daily Test and your step on this month's mountain. "Tip" brings a daily grammar tip from your own weak spots. Widgets show only what the app has saved on your device.

Daily reminders
One reminder a day at most, at the time you choose. Turn it on or off in Profile → Notifications.

Nine study buddies
Pick the character that climbs with you.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free includes one Daily Test and one practice session every day.
Premium unlocks Topic Practice across all ten topics and more practice sessions each day.
Premium is available as a monthly or annual subscription. Both include a free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

PRIVACY
There is no account and no sign-up. Your name, your progress and your practice history stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage data, never your name.
To give you feedback, the answers you type are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic. The app asks your permission before this happens for the first time, and you can withdraw it at any time from the Data screen.

Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Privacy Policy: https://ahmettayfur.com/products/grammarlens/privacy/
Terms of Service: https://ahmettayfur.com/products/grammarlens/terms/
```
