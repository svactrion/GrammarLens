### 1. The day's step before N01 — fixed

**Seen** (the tour, Premium, a second day): after the Daily Test, "See your
climb" brought Home with N01 already open over the mountain; the pawn walked
only after N01 was closed. Every new user meets this once, on the day of
their second Daily Test.

**Cause, confirmed before the change.** N01 is queued by the second test
(`_queueSoftAskAfterSecondTest`) and asked for on Home's first frames after
the results close. `_maybeShowSoftAsk` waited only for `_climbAnimating`,
which `_loadClimb` sets after several awaits (the progress read, the theme,
the month card, `endOfFrame`, the 250 ms scroll that brings the mountain
into view, the first-run zoom). On an ordinary day nothing else held N01
(`_restHeld` holds it only on a rest day), so it opened in that window. The
new test reproduced it before the fix: N01 on frame 21 (about 0.5 s after
"See your climb"), before the walk began.

**Fix** (`lib/screens/home_screen.dart`). A hold beside fix round 6's rest
wait: `_softAskWaitsForStep` is set when a Daily Test's completion is saved
(and for a pending climb handed to Home at start); `_maybeShowSoftAsk` stays
closed while it is set. When the pawn's arrival ends (`onArrivalEnd`: after
a save point's light and name) and nothing follows the step (no month card
waiting or open, no zoom), the same `HomeScreen.restBeat` (1 s, no new
number) runs and then releases the hold. On a rest day the hold is released
with the rest scene (`_endRestWait`), which N01 follows as before. It is
released at once when there is no step to show (no step earned, a step of
another month) or the climb fails to load, and when the user leaves Home
once the walk has begun, so N01 is never lost for the install. The release
and the opening of N01 each request a frame: after the beat's timer nothing
else may draw one (R-10).

**Tests** (`test/fix7_soft_ask_after_step_test.dart`, 10): a second day,
the test first, free and Premium (N01 not on any frame before the walk, not

### 2. Sheets follow an appearance change — fixed

**Seen** (the tour): with N01 or the length picker open, the system switched
from light to dark; the sheet kept its cream background while its text took
the dark theme: the title and "Not now" almost unreadable until it closed.

**Cause.** `showAppSheet` (`lib/widgets/app_sheet.dart`) passed the opener's
