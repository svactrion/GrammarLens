## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-23)

### D1. Interface languages

Seven interface languages are added: Japanese (`ja`), Korean (`ko`),
Spanish (`es`; one translation for Mexico and Spain), Brazilian Portuguese
(`pt-BR`), Turkish (`tr`), Arabic (`ar`, right to left) and German (`de`).
English stays the default and the fallback.

India gets no interface language of its own. The English store text is
tuned for IELTS instead; which App Store Connect localization India's
store shows is checked in Phase 4.

**Why:** the chosen markets combine low-to-mid English levels, a large
iPhone share and high App Store spending. China, Hong Kong and Russia are
out of scope because they are regions Anthropic does not support.

### D2. Questions and AI explanations stay in English

The questions and every AI explanation (Daily Test explanations, practice
feedback) stay in English in every interface language.

**Why:** the target level is B1–C1; the quality of AI content in seven
languages cannot be verified; cost. After the release, practice completion
rate is compared by language and this decision is reviewed.

### D3. Language-neutral proxy prompts

The proxy prompts stop assuming a Turkish learner. "Turkish learners",
"Turkish native speaker" and "Turkish keyboard" become "B1–C1 learners
whose first language is not English", and the keyboard tolerance becomes
keyboard-independent (accents, autocorrect, spelling).

Prompts affected (`proxy/src/anthropic.ts` as of `1126528`): practice
generation (`GENERATION_SYSTEM_PROMPT`), scoring
(`SCORING_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, the "Turkish keyboard" paragraph), the shared
Daily Test generator (`SHARED_DAILY_TEST_SYSTEM_PROMPT_V2`) and its checker
(`SHARED_CHECK_SYSTEM_PROMPT`).

**Why:** with seven interface languages the learner's first language is no
longer Turkish. **Condition:** the Daily Test prompt reaches every live
user at once (one shared set per date), so its quality is measured before
it is deployed.

### D4. Practice model measurement

With D3's prompts, `claude-sonnet-4-6` (the practice model today,
`proxy/src/anthropic.ts:24`) and `claude-sonnet-5-5` are measured on the
same test set. For scoring, `low` and `medium` effort are tried too. The
report gives, as a separate line, the difference in counting a user's
answer as right or wrong.

**Why:** the move to a language-neutral prompt is a natural point to
re-measure the model. The choice is the owner's; the expectation is a
move to `claude-sonnet-5-5`.

### D5. Widget

One small home-screen widget (WidgetKit). Content: the avatar, this
month's Monthly Climb step ("12 / 31"), and whether today's Daily Test is
done. No streak: Monthly Climb does not punish a missed day. The widget's
text is translated into the seven languages. Later versions add more.

**Why:** a reminder on the home screen without a streak's pressure,
consistent with Monthly Climb's design.

### D6. Translation method

Translations are made by AI only, with no native-speaker review (owner
decision). Feature names stay in English in every language: Daily Test,
Topic Practice, Review, Monthly Climb, Premium.

For the paywall, the AI permission screen and onboarding's "Your data &
AI" text, a back-translation check: in a separate step, the translation is
translated back into English and compared with the original.

The roadmap marks the translations "AI translation, no native review".

**Why:** the owner's decision on cost and speed; the back-translation
check covers the texts where a wrong meaning has legal or App Review
weight (prices and trials, what is sent to the AI service).

### D7. P14 now, deployed right after 1.3.0 is live

P14 (other accepted answers on the live shared sets, server side) is
developed now and deployed right after 1.3.0 is live. **Rule:** no proxy
deploy while 1.3.0 is in App Store review.

**Why:** P14 is server side and independent of an app release (roadmap,
"Moved out of 1.1.0"); the review rule keeps the reviewed build's backend
unchanged while it is being tested.
